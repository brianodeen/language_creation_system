# 7. Law, Justice & Authority

## Grammatical Mapping & Analysis
When translating this category into a target language, conlangers and linguists should establish the following grammatical features:
1. **Deontic Modality**: Define verbs, auxiliaries, or particles that express obligation, permission, or prohibition (e.g., "must do", "may enter", "shall not steal").
2. **Evidentiality Markers**: For legal statements, many conlangs require suffixes indicating how the speaker knows the information (e.g., "I saw it myself", "I heard it from a neighbor", "I infer it from footprints").
3. **Passive & Impersonal Constructions**: Create syntax for formal declarations where the agent is omitted or generalized (e.g., "It is decreed that...", "The thief was arrested").
4. **Legal Nominalization**: Establish suffix systems that transform verbs of action into legal concepts (e.g., *to judge* $\rightarrow$ *judgment/verdict*, *to steal* $\rightarrow$ *theft*).

## Subcategories & Dialogues

### 7.1 Stopped by the Guard
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (City Guard)**: Halt. The city gates are closed after the third bell. Show me your identification papers.
- **Speaker B (Traveler)**: Forgive me, officer. My cart lost a wheel on the north highway. I am a citizen here.
- **Speaker A (City Guard)**: Step forward into the light. Let me check the registry.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Officer)**: Why are you carrying a concealed iron blade inside the sanctuary?
- **Speaker B (Citizen)**: It is a ceremonial gift for the high priest. I did not mean to violate the law.

### 7.2 Reporting a Crime
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Citizen)**: I wish to report a theft! My horse was taken from the paddock during the night.
- **Speaker B (Clerk)**: Did you see the culprit? What was the description of the animal?
- **Speaker A (Citizen)**: No, but the fence was cut. The horse is a grey mare with a scar on her flank.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Shopkeeper)**: Guard! That thief stole a copper kettle from my shelf!
- **Speaker B (City Guard)**: Which way did they run? I will pursue them down the alley.

### 7.3 Collecting Evidence & Statements
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Investigator)**: Describe exactly what you saw when the merchant was attacked.
- **Speaker B (Witness)**: I heard a shout, and when I looked, a tall figure in a green cloak was running toward the docks.
- **Speaker A (Investigator)**: Did you see any weapon in their hands?
- **Speaker B (Witness)**: A short dagger, silver-hilted. I saw it catch the torchlight.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Officer)**: There are boot prints in the damp clay near the broken window.
- **Speaker B (Investigator)**: Take the measurements. They match the suspect's left shoe.

### 7.4 Trial Testimony
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Magistrate)**: Do you swear by the sacred law to speak the absolute truth?
- **Speaker B (Witness)**: I swear by the sky and the stone.
- **Speaker A (Magistrate)**: Tell the court where you were on the night of the solstice.
- **Speaker B (Witness)**: I was dining at the harbor inn, Master. I did not leave until midnight.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Accused)**: I am innocent of these charges. I was elsewhere.
- **Speaker B (Accuser)**: He lies! Three witnesses saw him hold the sack of stolen silver.

### 7.5 Guard Interrogating a Suspect
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Lieutenant)**: We found these gold tablets in your bedroll. They match the description of the items stolen from the temple treasury.
- **Speaker B (Suspect)**: I bought them from a peddler at the market. I did not know they were holy.
- **Speaker A (Lieutenant)**: Name this peddler, or you will take the blame.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Guard)**: Why was your cloak covered in mud if you stayed indoors?
- **Speaker B (Suspect)**: I... I slipped in the garden. I did not walk the road.

### 7.6 Appealing a Verdict
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Advocate)**: Your Grace, we appeal the magistrate's ruling. The fine is double the legal maximum.
- **Speaker B (Ruler)**: The magistrate acted under the new emergency decree. The appeal is denied.
- **Speaker A (Advocate)**: The decree does not apply to non-residents, Your Grace. Here is the charter.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Prisoner)**: I demand a trial by the high council.
- **Speaker B (Guard)**: You have no right to appeal, rebel. Your sentence is final.

### 7.7 Tax, Tribute & Fine Collections
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Tax Collector)**: By order of the governor, your household tax is four copper pieces for this quarter.
- **Speaker B (Homeowner)**: The harvest was poor, sir. Can I pay half now and half next month?
- **Speaker A (Tax Collector)**: I can grant a two-week extension, but no longer. Sign here.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Official)**: You were caught dumping waste in the public canal. The fine is ten coppers.
- **Speaker B (Citizen)**: Here is the silver equivalent. Forgive the offense.

### 7.8 Prison Guard & Prisoner Interactions
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Prisoner)**: Water... please. My cup is empty.
- **Speaker B (Guard)**: Quiet in the cells! You get water twice a day. The next bell is at noon.
- **Speaker A (Prisoner)**: Have mercy, I have a fever.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Guard)**: Step back from the bars. You have a visitor. Five minutes only.
- **Speaker B (Prisoner)**: Thank you. Is my brother outside?

### 7.9 Settling Property Disputes
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Neighbor A)**: Your stone wall encroaches on my pasture by three paces.
- **Speaker B (Neighbor B)**: The boundary marker was set by the surveyor ten years ago. It has not moved.
- **Speaker A (Neighbor A)**: Let us consult the public land deeds at the archive.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Buyer)**: I paid for the field, but you did not transfer the water rights.
- **Speaker B (Seller)**: Water rights require a separate contract. Read the original scroll.

### 7.10 Executions, Banishments & Sentences
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Herald)**: For the crime of treason against the high city, the council sentences you to banishment.
- **Speaker B (Condemned)**: I accept my exile. But the truth will return to these walls.
- **Speaker A (Herald)**: Strip him of his crest. Escort him beyond the outer border.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Judge)**: You are sentenced to five years of hard labor in the silver mines.
- **Speaker B (Convict)**: Have mercy, Master! My children will starve.
- **Speaker A (Judge)**: The law is set. Take him away.
