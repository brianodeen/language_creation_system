# 21. Underworld, Smuggling & Outlaw Operations

## Grammatical Mapping & Analysis
When translating this category into a target language, conlangers and linguists should establish the following grammatical features:
1. **Cryptic Slang Nominalization (Cant)**: Criminal groups use code words. Define a suffix system or vocabulary shifts that mask the true meaning of nouns (e.g., *gold* $\rightarrow$ *yellow-dust*, *guard* $\rightarrow$ *blue-coat*).
2. **Conditional Threats**: Formulate grammatical rules for threats and coercion (e.g., "Pay the fine or you will find your house ablaze").
3. **Dubitative Aspect (Plausible Deniability)**: Outlaw talk avoids direct statements. Use dubitative prefixes indicating that the speaker has no concrete knowledge, keeping statements speculative (e.g., "A shipment might-possibly arrive").
4. **Passive Agent Omission**: Establish syntax where active subjects are omitted to protect identities (e.g., "The wall was breached" instead of "Jack cut the wire").

## Subcategories & Dialogues

### 21.1 Black Market Contraband
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Buyer)**: Do you have the refined sulfur? The city watch has banned all sales.
- **Speaker B (Smuggler)**: I have three crates hidden under the coal sacks. It is costly due to the gate search.
- **Speaker A (Buyer)**: Name your price. I have gold bits ready.
- **Speaker B (Smuggler)**: Forty bits per crate. Take it or leave it.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Buyer)**: I need an imperial transit stamp.
- **Speaker B (Fence)**: The stamp is original. Ten silvers, and do not use it at the main gate.

### 21.2 Fencing Stolen Goods
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Thief)**: I have this silver necklace from the Duke's villa. The crest has been melted off.
- **Speaker B (Fence)**: Decent work, but the silver is low quality. I can give you five copper coins.
- **Speaker A (Thief)**: Five? The ruby in the center is genuine!
- **Speaker B (Fence)**: The ruby is hot. I must pay the cutter to recut it. Eight coppers is my limit.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Outlaw)**: Can you sell this cargo of silk?
- **Speaker B (Fence)**: If you deliver it to the harbor warehouse at midnight, I will take it.

### 21.3 Thieves' Cant & Secret Codes
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Rogue 1)**: Is the crow singing tonight?
- **Speaker B (Rogue 2)**: No, the cage is locked. The hounds are sniffing the alley.
- **Speaker A (Rogue 1)**: We must take the high path over the tiles instead.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Spy)**: The weather is windy in the south.
- **Speaker B (Contact)**: But the rain stays in the valley. The package is safe.

### 21.4 Bribing Officials
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Smuggler)**: Lieutenant, my cart has some extra bottles of spiced wine. I would hate to carry them back.
- **Speaker B (Guard Lieutenant)**: The import tax is five coppers, merchant.
- **Speaker A (Smuggler)**: Here are ten coppers... for your trouble. You can keep the wine.
- **Speaker B (Guard Lieutenant)**: The road looks clear today. Move along.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Prisoner)**: If you open the cell door, this gold ring is yours.
- **Speaker B (Guard)**: Leave the ring on the water tray. Walk out through the stables.

### 21.5 Heist Planning
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Rogue A)**: The vault door is guarded by two sentries. They rotate at the fourth bell.
- **Speaker B (Rogue B)**: We have three minutes to bypass the lock before the relief guard arrives.
- **Speaker A (Rogue A)**: I will disable the alarm relay. You handle the pins.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Leader)**: Who is our exit driver?
- **Speaker B (Outlaw)**: Old Thomas. He has a fast carriage waiting in the alley.

### 21.6 Smuggling Routes
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Smuggler 1)**: The watch has placed a guard post at the stone bridge.
- **Speaker B (Smuggler 2)**: We will use the old dry creek bed. The path is rough, but there are no watch towers.
- **Speaker A (Smuggler 1)**: Are the mules quiet enough?
- **Speaker B (Smuggler 2)**: We have tied their mouths.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Smuggler)**: The tunnel bypasses the city wall.
- **Speaker B (Merchant)**: The exit is hidden inside the baker's cellar.

### 21.7 Gang Territory & Alliances
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Gang Boss 1)**: The harbor docks belong to the Dock-Rats. Your crew has been selling spice at dock three.
- **Speaker B (Gang Boss 2)**: We pay the harbor master his share.
- **Speaker A (Gang Boss 1)**: The harbor master does not protect your throat in the alleys. Keep to the fish market or there will be blood.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Rogue)**: The Red Claws are proposing a truce.
- **Speaker B (Leader)**: Only if they return the stolen weapons from our warehouse.

### 21.8 Pirate Parleys
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Pirate Captain A)**: The merchant ship carried eighty silver bars. We share the booty equally.
- **Speaker B (Pirate Captain B)**: My ship took the brunt of the cannon fire. I demand forty percent.
- **Speaker A (Pirate Captain A)**: The parley rules state equal shares. Do not test my crew, brother.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Sailor)**: The target has raised the surrender flag.
- **Speaker B (Captain)**: Board the ship. Take the cargo and secure the hostages.

### 21.9 Coordinating Jailbreaks
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Conspirator 1)**: I have smuggled a steel file inside the loaf of bread.
- **Speaker B (Prisoner)**: Excellent. The guard is drunk on spiced wine tonight.
- **Speaker A (Conspirator 1)**: Cut the bars. I have a horse waiting at the south wall.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Rogue)**: We will start a fire at the barracks to distract the watch.
- **Speaker B (Prisoner)**: I will break the lock as soon as the alarm sounds.

### 21.10 Harboring Fugitives
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Outlaw)**: The watch is searching every house in the quarter. Where can I hide?
- **Speaker B (Ally)**: Get into the space behind the hearth wall. The stone is hollow.
- **Speaker A (Outlaw)**: I hear boots in the street!
- **Speaker B (Ally)**: Crawl in now. I will cover the entrance with this cabinet.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Guard)**: Have you seen this rebel?
- **Speaker B (Citizen)**: No, officer. I have been alone all morning.
