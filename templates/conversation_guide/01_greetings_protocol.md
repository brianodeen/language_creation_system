# 1. Greetings, Protocol & Etiquette

## Grammatical Mapping & Analysis
When translating this category into a target language, conlangers and linguists should establish the following grammatical features:
1. **Honorific Registers**: Determine if the verb endings or pronoun selections change based on relative social status (e.g., Addressing a King vs. Addressing a peasant).
2. **Vocative Case**: Define how nouns change when they are being addressed directly (e.g., "O King!", "my friend").
3. **Politeness Markers**: Create particles or suffixes that signify deference, apology, or request.
4. **Pronoun Indexing**: Establish singular vs. plural second-person forms ("thou" vs. "you") and inclusive vs. exclusive first-person plurals ("we including you" vs. "we excluding you").

## Subcategories & Dialogues

### 1.1 Formal Introductions
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Diplomat)**: Greetings, honored emissary. I am Lord Alistair of the Western Reach.
- **Speaker B (Emissary)**: It is a privilege to meet you, Lord Alistair. I represent the High Council.
- **Speaker A (Diplomat)**: May our discussions bring prosperity to both our lands.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Scholar)**: Allow me to introduce myself. I am Master Eldon, Chief Archivist of the Citadel.
- **Speaker B (Novice)**: The honor is mine, Master Eldon. I have read your treatises on ancient grammar.
- **Speaker A (Scholar)**: You flatter me. Let us begin our tour of the archives.

### 1.2 Informal Greetings
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Friend 1)**: Hey there! Good to see you.
- **Speaker B (Friend 2)**: Hey! It's been way too long. How have you been?
- **Speaker A (Friend 1)**: Not bad, just busy. You heading to the market?
- **Speaker B (Friend 2)**: Yeah, let's walk together.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Merchant)**: Mornin' traveler. Cold wind today, eh?
- **Speaker B (Traveler)**: Truly. Do you have hot tea brewing?
- **Speaker A (Merchant)**: Right over by the hearth. Grab a cup.

### 1.3 Partings & Farewells
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Parent)**: Goodbye, my child. Safe travels on the road.
- **Speaker B (Child)**: Farewell, father. I will return before the winter snows.
- **Speaker A (Parent)**: May the spirits watch over your path.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Shopkeeper)**: Thank you for your custom. Have a good evening.
- **Speaker B (Customer)**: Goodbye. See you next week.
- **Speaker A (Shopkeeper)**: Until next time!

### 1.4 Royal & Court Etiquette
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Herald)**: Presenting Her Royal Highness, Queen Yvaine.
- **Speaker B (Knight)**: I bow before your grace. I bring urgent news from the borderlands.
- **Speaker A (Queen)**: Rise, Sir Thomas. Speak, and hide nothing from us.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Courtier)**: Remember to bow at the waist, and do not speak until the Emperor addresses you.
- **Speaker B (Diplomat)**: I understand. I shall observe the traditional protocols.

### 1.5 Showing Deference
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Apprentice)**: Forgive my interruption, Master, but I have completed the transcription.
- **Speaker B (Master)**: Well done. Place it on the desk and return to your studies.
- **Speaker A (Apprentice)**: As you wish, Master.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Soldier)**: General, the watch towers report no movement.
- **Speaker B (General)**: Good. Keep the watch alert. Dismissed.
- **Speaker A (Soldier)**: Yes, General.

### 1.6 Welcoming Guests
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Host)**: Welcome to our home! Please, come inside out of the rain.
- **Speaker B (Guest)**: Thank you for your kind hospitality. It is a relief to find shelter.
- **Speaker A (Host)**: Let me take your wet cloak. Warm food is on the table.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Innkeeper)**: Welcome, weary travelers. We have fresh straw in the stables and hot stew in the kettle.
- **Speaker B (Traveler)**: A welcome sight indeed. We require rooms for three nights.

### 1.7 Inquiring about Health
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Neighbor 1)**: Good day! How is your mother feeling? Has the fever broken?
- **Speaker B (Neighbor 2)**: Yes, thank you. She slept through the night and is regaining her strength.
- **Speaker A (Neighbor 1)**: That is wonderful news. Let me know if you need any herbs.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Elder)**: You look pale, young one. Are you well?
- **Speaker B (Youth)**: Just a bit tired from the long journey, elder. I will be fine after some rest.

### 1.8 Accidental Encounters
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Traveler 1)**: Wait... Julian? Is that you?
- **Speaker B (Traveler 2)**: By the gods! I didn't expect to run into you in a place like this.
- **Speaker A (Traveler 1)**: What are you doing so far from the valley?
- **Speaker B (Traveler 2)**: I'm trading goods. Let's sit and catch up.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Guard)**: Halt! Oh, it is you, Scholar. Pardon the alarm.
- **Speaker B (Scholar)**: No harm done, Lieutenant. I was lost in my calculations.

### 1.9 Apologies & Reconciliation
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Colleague 1)**: Please accept my apologies. I did not mean to offend you during the council meeting.
- **Speaker B (Colleague 2)**: It is forgotten. We both care deeply about the colony's safety.
- **Speaker A (Colleague 1)**: Thank you. Let us work together on the next draft.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Merchant)**: I am sorry for the delay in your shipment.
- **Speaker B (Guildmaster)**: Your apology is accepted, but ensure it does not happen again.

### 1.10 Seasonal Blessings
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Priest)**: May the new spring sun bring bounty to your fields.
- **Speaker B (Farmer)**: And may the sky remain gentle for your rituals.
- **Speaker A (Priest)**: Blessed solstice to you and your kin.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Citizen 1)**: Happy festival of lights!
- **Speaker B (Citizen 2)**: May your candles burn bright throughout the long night.
