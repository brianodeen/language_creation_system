# 25. Xenolinguistics, Monsters & Spirit Parley

## Grammatical Mapping & Analysis
When translating this category into a target language, conlangers and linguists should establish the following grammatical features:
1. **Translation Mismatch Particles**: Fictional/non-human languages have concepts that do not translate cleanly. Create discourse particles that indicate translation limits, errors, or untranslatable context (e.g., "concept-missing-error", "approximate-translation").
2. **Psych-Verbs (Telepathy)**: Telepathic or mental communication bypasses vocal paths. Define a set of mental perception verbs that indicate direct thought-linkage (e.g., "I thought-project to you", "mind-feels").
3. **Inverse Alignment**: Many fantasy and sci-fi languages use an inverse alignment pattern where the relative status of the interactants determines the agent vs. patient mapping (e.g., a lesser mortal must occupy the grammatical object position when addressed by a dragon or ancient spirit).
4. **Pronoun Shifts for Non-Sapients**: Outline distinct pronoun choices for non-human, hive-mind, or spiritual entities.

## Subcategories & Dialogues

### 25.1 Universal Translator Failures
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Diplomat)**: The translator is outputting static. The alien grammar has too many spatial dimensions.
- **Speaker B (Tech)**: Switch the analyzer to vector-mode. Let it map the pitch accent variations.
- **Speaker A (Diplomat)**: It is compiling... I am getting a partial match: "We offer... greeting... or warning?"

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Alien)**: [Clicking noises]
- **Speaker B (Explorer)**: The translator says: "Do not stand on our nests." Step off the green grass!

### 25.2 Dragon Parley
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Dragon)**: Why does this tiny creature crawl into my vault? Do you seek the gold, or death?
- **Speaker B (Hero)**: I seek neither, ancient one. I seek the sword of my father, which was lost in your hoard.
- **Speaker A (Dragon)**: The sword is mine now. What do you offer to buy it back?
- **Speaker B (Hero)**: The location of the hidden dragon-nest in the southern ridge.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Dragon)**: I have watched your kings rise and fall like grass. Your treaties mean nothing to me.
- **Speaker B (Diplomat)**: We offer iron ore and a pledge of peace.

### 25.3 Negotiating with Undead
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Priest)**: Rest, spirit! Your bones are buried. Why do you haunt the cellar?
- **Speaker B (Wraith)**: The covenant was broken. The family seal was stolen from my tomb.
- **Speaker A (Priest)**: We will find the seal and return it. Rest until the next moon.
- **Speaker B (Wraith)**: I will wait... but if the seal is not returned, the house will burn.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Necromancer)**: Speak, bone-walker. Where is the Duke's ring?
- **Speaker B (Zombie)**: Hidden... under the stone altar.

### 25.4 Bargaining with Elementals
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Mage)**: Spirit of the Flood, we request you to lower the river levels. The fields are drowning.
- **Speaker B (Water Elemental)**: The water must flow. If I lower the river, the lake will dry.
- **Speaker A (Mage)**: We will open the flood gates to the east to guide the excess water.
- **Speaker B (Water Elemental)**: Agreed. Open the gates, and I will recede.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Sorcerer)**: Fire spirit, heat this forge.
- **Speaker B (Fire Elemental)**: Feed me red charcoal, and I will burn white-hot.

### 25.5 Hive-Mind Telepathy
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Mind)**: We are many, yet we speak as one. Why does the individual intrude on our thoughts?
- **Speaker B (Explorer)**: I seek passage through your tunnels. I carry no weapons.
- **Speaker A (Mind)**: We feel your fear. Your thoughts are noisy. Pass quickly, before the swarm reacts.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Explorer)**: Can you hear my voice?
- **Speaker B (Hive-Mind)**: We do not hear voices. We perceive your chemical signals.

### 25.6 Subterranean Trades
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Merchant)**: We offer wheat and dried fruit in exchange for your worked iron.
- **Speaker B (Goblincraft Master)**: Wheat is good, but we want copper ore too. Our smiths need raw material.
- **Speaker A (Merchant)**: Two wagonloads of copper, and ten bags of wheat.
- **Speaker B (Goblincraft Master)**: Done. Deliver them to the cave mouth at sunset.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Dwarf)**: The price of this gold ring is fifty silvers.
- **Speaker B (Customer)**: That is too high. I will pay forty.

### 25.7 Alien First Contact
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Captain)**: We represent the United Systems. We come in peace.
- **Speaker B (Alien Envoy)**: [Colors flashing on skin]
- **Speaker A (Captain)**: The color patterns indicate agreement. Show them the treaty tablet.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Explorer)**: Do not touch their limbs. The touch is a sign of combat.
- **Speaker B (Companion)**: Understood. Standing back.

### 25.8 Warding Off Malevolent Apparitions
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Priest)**: "By the light of the sun, I banish you, shadow-fiend! Leave this child!"
- **Speaker B (Demon)**: The child is mine! You have no power here!
- **Speaker A (Priest)**: The circle of salt is drawn. You cannot cross. Depart!

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Traveler)**: The red eyes are watching us from the trees.
- **Speaker B (Guide)**: Burn the holy leaf. The scent will drive them away.

### 25.9 Soothing an Enraged Beast-Monster
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Tamer)**: Stand down, great beast. No one will hurt you.
- **Speaker B (Monster)**: [Growling and stamping]
- **Speaker A (Tamer)**: Low voice... offer the honey-root. Keep your movements slow.
- **Speaker B (Monster)**: [Sniffing the root... growling stops]

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Companion)**: The griffin is raising its wings!
- **Speaker B (Tamer)**: Lower your head. Show your neck. It is the sign of submission.

### 25.10 Telepathic Mind-Links
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Sender)**: *Can you hear my thoughts? I am trapped in the lower dungeon.*
- **Speaker B (Receiver)**: *I hear you. The connection is faint. Are the guards alert?*
- **Speaker A (Sender)**: *Only one sentry. Bring the file and the horse.*

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Sender)**: *I am sending you the map image now.*
- **Speaker B (Receiver)**: *I see it. The cavern entrance is near the waterfall.*
