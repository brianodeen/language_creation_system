# 24. Supernatural, Occult & Magical Arts

## Grammatical Mapping & Analysis
When translating this category into a target language, conlangers and linguists should establish the following grammatical features:
1. **Jussive Mood (Indirect Commands)**: Magic incantations rely on commands directed at objects, natural forces, or third parties (e.g., "Let the flame rise!", "May the water freeze!"). Establish jussive verb forms.
2. **Subjunctive Mood (Hypothetical Outcomes)**: Define the grammar for spells that conditionalize reality (e.g., "Were I to release the rune, the seal would break").
3. **Sacred vs. Profane Cases**: Specify if nouns or verbs change cases when interacting with supernatural or magical concepts (e.g., "strike *with a sword*" vs. "strike *with a magical blast*").
4. **Magical Focus Instrumentals**: Define how to mark the instrument through which magic flows (e.g., "cast *through the staff*", "channel *via the crystal*").

## Subcategories & Dialogues

### 24.1 Spellcasting Incantations
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Mage)**: Stand back. The spell requires absolute focus. "Let the wind gather, let the storm break!"
- **Speaker B (Apprentice)**: The air is glowing blue! The runes are lighting up!
- **Speaker A (Mage)**: The seal is open. Enter the vault.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Sorcerer)**: "May this shield block the arrow, may this iron stand true!"
- **Speaker B (Target)**: I feel the shield barrier. It is solid like stone.

### 24.2 Brewing Alchemical Potions
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Alchemist)**: Add three crushed petals of the fire-lily to the boiling solution.
- **Speaker B (Apprentice)**: The color is changing from blue to deep orange. The steam smells like cinnamon.
- **Speaker A (Alchemist)**: Good. Stir it counter-clockwise until the bubbling stops.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Student)**: How do we neutralize this toxic vapor?
- **Speaker B (Alchemist)**: Add a pinch of salt-ash to the flask.

### 24.3 Summoning Spirits
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Summoner)**: "By the blood and the bone, I summon thee, Spirit of the Hearth!"
- **Speaker B (Spirit)**: Who calls me from the long sleep? What do you seek, mortal?
- **Speaker A (Summoner)**: We seek your guidance to locate the lost well of our ancestors.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Mage)**: The spirit is hostile! The wards are cracking!
- **Speaker B (Apprentice)**: I am reinforcing the salt circle now!

### 24.4 Enchanting Wares
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Enchanter)**: I am carving the rune of weightlessness onto this iron shield.
- **Speaker B (Client)**: Will it make the shield lighter to carry?
- **Speaker A (Enchanter)**: It will feel like wood, but it will block blows like steel.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Blacksmith)**: Can you imbue this sword with frost magic?
- **Speaker B (Enchanter)**: Yes, but I will require a pure sapphire to hold the enchantment.

### 24.5 Curses & Hexes
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Victim)**: I was cursed by the witch! My crops are rotting, and my livestock is sick.
- **Speaker B (Healer)**: We must find the source of the hex. It is usually a carved bone buried near your gate.
- **Speaker A (Victim)**: Help me dig it up, please.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Warlock)**: "Let darkness cover your eyes, let your tongue forget its words!"
- **Speaker B (Target)**: I... I cannot see! What have you done?

### 24.6 Drawing Wards & Magical Runes
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Mage)**: Draw the circle with silver chalk. Make sure there are no gaps in the outer rim.
- **Speaker B (Apprentice)**: The lines are straight, Master. Should I write the rune of binding in the center?
- **Speaker A (Mage)**: Yes, draw it slowly. A single mistake will break the seal.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Sentry)**: The ward is flashing red! An invisible creature is approaching the door.
- **Speaker B (Guard)**: Draw your weapons. Keep behind the line.

### 24.7 Leyline Attunement
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Mage)**: The magic is weak in this valley. We are far from the main leylines.
- **Speaker B (Apprentice)**: Can we locate a junction point with the copper rod?
- **Speaker A (Mage)**: Yes, look for the place where the needles spin in circles. The energy is concentrated there.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Sorcerer)**: I feel the current of the earth element rising through my boots.
- **Speaker B (Companion)**: Your eyes are glowing green. The attunement is complete.

### 24.8 Authenticating Divine Relics
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Scholar)**: This bone relic is claimed to belong to the first saint.
- **Speaker B (Priest)**: Pass your hand over the container. Do you feel the cool hum of the sacred energy?
- **Speaker A (Scholar)**: Yes, it is unmistakable. The relic is genuine.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Buyer)**: Is this talisman truly blessed by the Sun Priest?
- **Speaker B (Seller)**: The stamp on the gold loop is original. Feel the warmth.

### 24.9 Necromancy & Undead Alarms

#### Conversation 2

### 24.10 Magical Academy Code of Conduct
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Dean)**: In this academy, all casting of fire spells outside the training chambers is strictly forbidden.
- **Speaker B (Student)**: It was an accident, Dean. My wand slipped.
- **Speaker A (Dean)**: An accident can burn down the library. Your wand is confiscated for a week.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Instructor)**: Read chapter three on rune safety before you enter the forge.
- **Speaker B (Novice)**: I have memorized the safety steps, Master.
