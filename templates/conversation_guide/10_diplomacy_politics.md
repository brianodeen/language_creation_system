# 10. Diplomacy, Treaties & Politics

## Grammatical Mapping & Analysis
When translating this category into a target language, conlangers and linguists should establish the following grammatical features:
1. **Passive Honorific Verb Forms**: High-level political discourse avoids direct accusations or commands. Establish indirect verb structures (e.g., "The decision has been observed to occur" instead of "You made a decision").
2. **Dubitative Mood**: Create a grammatical category used to indicate uncertainty, gossip, or unconfirmed intelligence reports (e.g., "It is rumored-that...", "They might-have-signed the pact").
3. **Formal Conjunctives & Discourse Connectors**: Develop complex sentence transition particles (e.g., "furthermore", "nonetheless", "notwithstanding our agreement").
4. **Reciprocal Verb Affixes**: Formulate grammatical markers indicating mutual action between parties (e.g., "to trade-mutually", "to align-together").

## Subcategories & Dialogues

### 10.1 Audience with Sovereigns
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Herald)**: Step forward and present your petitions to the Council.
- **Speaker B (Diplomat)**: Gracious Lords, I represent the free cities of the coast. We request protection against the northern incursions.
- **Speaker A (Councilor)**: What do the coast cities offer in exchange for our shields?
- **Speaker B (Diplomat)**: We offer tariff-free access to our deep-water ports.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Chancellor)**: The Emperor has granted you three minutes to speak. Bow at the dais.
- **Speaker B (Envoy)**: I am grateful for His Majesty's mercy.

### 10.2 Negotiating Alliances
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Envoy A)**: Our kingdoms must stand together. If the iron legions cross the border, neither of us can survive alone.
- **Speaker B (Envoy B)**: We agree, but our army cannot march unless you guarantee grain supplies for the winter.
- **Speaker A (Envoy A)**: We will supply twenty cargo ships of wheat as soon as the pact is signed.
- **Speaker B (Envoy B)**: Then let us summon the scribes to draft the alliance.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Leader)**: Will you join our coalition against the merchant guild?
- **Speaker B (Allied Chief)**: On one condition: our representatives must hold a seat on the trade council.

### 10.3 Boundary Disputes
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Negotiator 1)**: The ancient boundary line runs along the crest of the ridge, not the river valley.
- **Speaker B (Negotiator 2)**: Your maps ignore the shift in the river's flow over the last century. Our farmers have plowed the valley for generations.
- **Speaker A (Negotiator 1)**: We propose a demilitarized zone in the valley, with shared access to the water.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Officer)**: Your patrol crossed the border markers.
- **Speaker B (Sentry)**: The markers were obscured by the landslide. We will fall back to the ridge.

### 10.4 Treaty Drafting
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Scribe)**: Scribe, write this down: "Clause four: All prisoners of war shall be returned within thirty days of ratification."
- **Speaker B (Diplomat A)**: And add: "Without ransom or conditions."
- **Speaker A (Scribe)**: Document updated. Let the representatives apply their wax seals.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Legal Expert)**: The term "reasonable access" is too vague. We must define the exact transit corridors.
- **Speaker B (Diplomat)**: Agreed. List the specific roads and gate coordinates.

### 10.5 Diplomatic Gift Exchanges
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Sovereign A)**: We present this gold-embossed astronomical sphere as a token of our shared search for truth.
- **Speaker B (Sovereign B)**: It is a work of pure genius. In return, please accept these three bolts of sea-silk from our southern weavers.
- **Speaker A (Sovereign A)**: May these gifts seal our friendship for generations.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Envoy)**: I bring these rare spices from our king.
- **Speaker B (Ruler)**: Your king is generous. Tell him his messengers are always welcome in my halls.

### 10.6 Court Intrigue & Rumors
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Courtier 1)**: Have you heard? The Duke is whispering with the northern delegates behind locked doors.
- **Speaker B (Courtier 2)**: He seeks the governorship of the borderlands. He will betray the Chancellor if the price is right.
- **Speaker A (Courtier 1)**: We must warn the Guard before the Duke signs his secret pact.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Noble)**: The Chancellor's health is failing.
- **Speaker B (Senator)**: Yes, the succession crisis is imminent. We must align our votes.

### 10.7 Espionage Briefings
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Spymaster)**: What information did our contact in the capital provide?
- **Speaker B (Agent)**: The troop deployments are a diversion. The real assault will target the coastal tunnels.
- **Speaker A (Spymaster)**: Did they provide the activation codes?
- **Speaker B (Agent)**: Yes, they are recorded on this encrypted memory chip.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Agent)**: I was followed. The city watch is monitoring the docks.
- **Speaker B (Spymaster)**: Change your cloak and leave the city via the sewer tunnels.

### 10.8 Political Debates
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Senator A)**: The proposed tax on grain will ruin the smallholder farmers. The wealth resides in the merchant guild!
- **Speaker B (Senator B)**: The merchant guild pays for the navy that protects our shores from raiders. Everyone must contribute.
- **Speaker A (Senator A)**: If the farmers starve, who will eat the merchant's imports?

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Citizen A)**: The Council has sat for three terms with no results.
- **Speaker B (Citizen B)**: We need a new election, or there will be riots in the streets.

### 10.9 Public Proclamations
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Town Crier)**: Hear ye, hear ye! By decree of the High Senate, a curfew is declared starting at the fourth bell tonight.
- **Speaker B (Citizen)**: Curfew? Why? What has happened?
- **Speaker A (Town Crier)**: All gates are barred. Violators will be arrested as collaborators.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Herald)**: The treaty has been signed! The war is ended!
- **Speaker B (Crowd Member)**: Praise the skies! Peace at last!

### 10.10 Trade Agreement Accords
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Merchant Guild)**: We propose a reduction of tariffs on raw lumber to three percent.
- **Speaker B (Representative)**: Only if your guild restricts lumber exports during the building season. Our local builders need materials.
- **Speaker A (Merchant Guild)**: Agreed. We will sign the timber accord.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Collector)**: The trade treaty exempts this shipment from import duties.
- **Speaker B (Official)**: Verify the cargo seal. Yes, it is the guild seal. Pass them through.
