# 2. Trade, Commerce & Haggling

## Grammatical Mapping & Analysis
When translating this category into a target language, conlangers and linguists should establish the following grammatical features:
1. **Comparative and Superlative Adjectives**: Define how to modify adjectives to express value or quality (e.g., "cheap", "cheaper", "cheapest", "costly").
2. **Numeral Classifiers**: Many languages require specific markers when counting physical objects based on their shape or utility (e.g., "three circular coins", "five long swords").
3. **Dative & Benefactive Cases**: Specify the grammatical marker for the recipient of the trade (e.g., "give the gold *to the merchant*", "buy this *for my sister*").
4. **Conditional Syntax**: Construct templates for negotiation clauses (e.g., "If I pay ten coins, will you include the harness?").

## Subcategories & Dialogues

### 2.1 Initial Appraisals
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Buyer)**: What is the asking price for this woven rug?
- **Speaker B (Merchant)**: That is pure northern wool. I value it at fifty silver coins.
- **Speaker A (Buyer)**: It is fine work, but fifty is steep for this size.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Blacksmith)**: Let me see that dagger. The steel is decent, but the hilt is loose.
- **Speaker B (Seller)**: Can you repair it, blacksmith? What is it worth to you?
- **Speaker A (Blacksmith)**: In this state, I cannot give you more than three bronze bits.

### 2.2 Haggling & Barter
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Traveler)**: I will give you twelve copper pieces for this cloak.
- **Speaker B (Merchant)**: Twelve? You insult me! It is worth thirty at least.
- **Speaker A (Traveler)**: How about twenty, and I will add this flask of spiced oil?
- **Speaker B (Merchant)**: Make it twenty-five and the oil, and we have a deal.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Farmer)**: I offer two sacks of grain for that iron hoe.
- **Speaker B (Merchant)**: Make it three sacks, or two sacks and a copper piece.
- **Speaker A (Farmer)**: Three sacks it is. I will fetch them from my cart.

### 2.3 Buying Provisions
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Traveler)**: We need dried meat and hard bread for a ten-day journey.
- **Speaker B (Provisioner)**: I have salted beef and travel biscuits in stock. How many travelers?
- **Speaker A (Traveler)**: Four of us. Pack enough to keep us full.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Customer)**: Is this butter fresh?
- **Speaker B (Merchant)**: Churned this morning, straight from the dairy.
- **Speaker A (Customer)**: Excellent. I will take two blocks and a wheel of cheese.

### 2.4 Purchasing Specialty Gear
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Mercenary)**: I need a longsword, well-balanced, with a reinforced crossguard.
- **Speaker B (Armorer)**: I forged this one last week. High-carbon iron, sturdy tang.
- **Speaker A (Mercenary)**: Let me feel the balance. Ah, yes. What do you ask for it?

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Alchemist)**: Do you have refined sulfur and glass vials?
- **Speaker B (Herbalist)**: The sulfur is in the back room, and the vials are on the shelf.
- **Speaker A (Alchemist)**: I will take ten vials and three pounds of the powder.

### 2.5 Currency Exchange
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Merchant)**: I cannot accept these foreign gold coins. The stamp is unfamiliar.
- **Speaker B (Traveler)**: They are from the Eastern Empire. The weight is pure.
- **Speaker A (Merchant)**: You must go to the scale-master at the gate to verify the weight.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Traveler)**: What is the exchange rate between imperial silver and local copper?
- **Speaker B (Exchange Master)**: One silver coin will buy you twelve copper bits today.
- **Speaker A (Traveler)**: Very well. Exchange ten of my silvers, please.

### 2.6 Credit, Loans & Debts
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Borrower)**: I need a loan of one hundred silvers to purchase seed for the sowing.
- **Speaker B (Money Lender)**: I can provide it, but the return is one hundred and ten silvers by harvest.
- **Speaker A (Borrower)**: Agreed. I will sign the tablet.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Merchant)**: Your tab at this establishment is overdue, friend.
- **Speaker B (Patron)**: I know. My caravan was delayed. I will pay you double next week.

### 2.7 Negotiating Service Contracts
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Guildmaster)**: We need a team to clear the irrigation ditch by the solstice.
- **Speaker B (Labor Boss)**: That is hard clay. I will require ten men and five silver pieces a day.
- **Speaker A (Guildmaster)**: Four silvers a day, and we provide the shovels.
- **Speaker B (Labor Boss)**: Deal. We start tomorrow at dawn.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Author)**: I want a copy of this manuscript translated into the common tongue.
- **Speaker B (Scribe)**: It will take me two weeks. My fee is fifty coppers.

### 2.8 Customs Fees & Tolls
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Gate Guard)**: Halt. The cargo toll is two copper coins per laden beast.
- **Speaker B (Caravan Leader)**: I have five mules. Here are ten coppers.
- **Speaker A (Gate Guard)**: Pass through. Keep to the main thoroughfare.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Harbor Master)**: Your ship carries iron ore. The import duty is five percent of value.
- **Speaker B (Captain)**: Here is the manifest. The value is appraised at four hundred silvers.

### 2.9 Disputes Over Quality & Measure
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Buyer)**: This sack of grain is short! Your scales are rigged.
- **Speaker B (Merchant)**: Nonsense. The bag was weighed before the council inspector.
- **Speaker A (Buyer)**: Let us weigh it again on the public scale at the town hall.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Customer)**: This cloth is frayed along the edges. I want a refund.
- **Speaker B (Merchant)**: I do not offer refunds, but I will exchange it for a fresh roll.

### 2.10 Hiring Caravan Guards & Guild Labor
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Merchant)**: The road through the pass is infested with bandits. I need three swords.
- **Speaker B (Mercenary)**: We charge two silvers per day per man, plus food.
- **Speaker A (Merchant)**: Agreed. Half pay now, and half when we reach the coast.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Builder)**: I need three journeymen masons for the cathedral wall.
- **Speaker B (Guild Master)**: You must hire through the guild office. The rate is standard.
