# 3. Food, Dining & Hospitality

## Grammatical Mapping & Analysis
When translating this category into a target language, conlangers and linguists should establish the following grammatical features:
1. **Partitive Genitive Case**: Determine how to express portions of items (e.g., "a slice *of bread*", "some *of the water*").
2. **Optative Mood (Wishes & Toasting)**: Formulate grammatical markers used for expressing desires, toasts, or hopes during celebrations (e.g., "May you live long!", "Let us drink!").
3. **Sensory Verbs**: Standardize verbs of taste, smell, and visual appearance (e.g., "This tastes sweet", "The stew smells rich").
4. **Locative & Instrumental Postpositions**: Define where tableware and food items are placed relative to each other (e.g., "on the table", "with a spoon").

## Subcategories & Dialogues

### 3.1 Booking Inn Lodging
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Traveler)**: Good evening. Do you have a room available for two people?
- **Speaker B (Innkeeper)**: Yes, we have a double room upstairs. It is five coppers a night.
- **Speaker A (Traveler)**: Does that include stabling for our horses?
- **Speaker B (Innkeeper)**: Stabling is one extra copper per horse.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Traveler)**: I need a clean bed and a lock on the door.
- **Speaker B (Innkeeper)**: I have a small room at the end of the hall. Very quiet.

### 3.2 Ordering in a Tavern
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Patron)**: What is the kitchen serving today?
- **Speaker B (Tavern Keeper)**: Roast mutton with root vegetables, and fresh rye bread.
- **Speaker A (Patron)**: Bring me a plate of the mutton and a mug of dark ale.
- **Speaker B (Tavern Keeper)**: Coming right up.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Patron)**: A cup of hot water and some honey, please.
- **Speaker B (Tavern Keeper)**: Immediately. We have mountain honey available.

### 3.3 Paying the Bill
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Patron)**: Tavern keeper, I would like to settle my tab.
- **Speaker B (Innkeeper)**: Let me check. Two stews, three ales, and one night's room. That is thirteen coppers.
- **Speaker A (Patron)**: Here are fifteen. Keep the change for your excellent service.
- **Speaker B (Innkeeper)**: Thank you, traveler. May your road be smooth.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Guest)**: Is the tax included in this price?
- **Speaker B (Innkeeper)**: Yes, all city duties are already calculated.

### 3.4 Camp Kitchen Setup
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Scout)**: We need to set up the fire pit before the rain starts.
- **Speaker B (Hunter)**: I will dig the trench. You fetch the dry wood from under the tarp.
- **Speaker A (Scout)**: Where is the iron tripod for the kettle?
- **Speaker B (Hunter)**: Packed in the blue canvas sack.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Traveler)**: Who is on water duty tonight?
- **Speaker B (Traveler 2)**: It's my turn. I'll take the buckets down to the creek.

### 3.5 Foraged Prep & Cooking
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Cook)**: Are these mushrooms safe to add to the broth?
- **Speaker B (Forager)**: Yes, those are pine-caps. Very flavorful. Avoid the spotted red ones, though.
- **Speaker A (Cook)**: Slice them thin and toss them in with the salt meat.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Traveler)**: Stir the pot so the porridge doesn't burn on the bottom.
- **Speaker B (Traveler 2)**: I'm on it. Should I add the dried berries now?
- **Speaker A (Traveler)**: Yes, let them soften in the steam.

### 3.6 Feasts & Court Banquets
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Noble Guest)**: The Lord's table is magnificent tonight.
- **Speaker B (Dignitary)**: Indeed. The roasted boar was basted in wild plum wine.
- **Speaker A (Noble Guest)**: Pass the silver platter of spiced figs, please.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Servant)**: More wine for your cup, my lady?
- **Speaker B (Lady)**: Only a half-pour, thank you. Bring water as well.

### 3.7 Toasts & Celebrations
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Host)**: Raise your glasses, my friends! To the health of our new alliance.
- **Speaker B (Guests)**: To the alliance! May it stand a thousand years.
- **Speaker A (Host)**: Drink deep!

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Friend)**: To your safe return from the sea.
- **Speaker B (Captain)**: To the stars that guided us home. Cheers!

### 3.8 Dietary Restrictions & Allergies
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Traveler)**: Does this bread contain barley? It makes my stomach swell.
- **Speaker B (Host)**: No, this is pure oat flour. We baked it separately.
- **Speaker A (Traveler)**: That is a relief. I can eat it safely.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Guest)**: I cannot eat fish or shellfish.
- **Speaker B (Cook)**: I will prepare a vegetable dish with beans instead.

### 3.9 Complaining & Praising Quality
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Patron)**: This soup is completely cold, and the meat is tough.
- **Speaker B (Tavern Keeper)**: My apologies, traveler. Let me take it back to the hearth to heat.
- **Speaker A (Patron)**: Thank you. Please ensure the bread is warm too.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Guest)**: This is the best venison I have ever tasted!
- **Speaker B (Host)**: The chef smoked it over hickory wood. I am glad you enjoy it.

### 3.10 Offering Shelter to Strangers
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Farmer)**: A storm is rising, traveler. Do not try to cross the pass tonight.
- **Speaker B (Stranger)**: I have no coins to pay for shelter, farmer.
- **Speaker A (Farmer)**: You do not need coins. My hayloft is warm and dry. Come inside.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Traveler)**: We are lost and freezing. Can you help us?
- **Speaker B (Homestead Owner)**: Sit by the fire. We have warm broth and space by the hearth.
