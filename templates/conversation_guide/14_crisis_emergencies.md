# 14. Crisis, Disaster & Emergencies

## Grammatical Mapping & Analysis
When translating this category into a target language, conlangers and linguists should establish the following grammatical features:
1. **Urgent Imperative Truncation (Absolute Imperatives)**: In crisis situations, commands are stripped of polite particles, pronouns, and redundant verbs (e.g., "Fire!", "Jump!", "Run!"). Define how these short-form directives are structured.
2. **Spatial Orientation Verbs**: Create high-utility motion verbs that indicate directional urgency (e.g., "climb-upward-now", "retreat-inward").
3. **Passive-Impersonal Evidentials**: When reporting a disaster, define how to express external events where the cause is unknown or secondary to the event (e.g., "The wall collapsed", "smoke has been seen").
4. **Vocative Call for Help**: Design specific syntactic templates for calling for help or reinforcements (e.g., "Help!", "To me!").

## Subcategories & Dialogues

### 14.1 Fighting Fires
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Citizen)**: Fire! The kitchen chimney is ablaze! The roof is catching!
- **Speaker B (Volunteer)**: Grab the water buckets! Form a line to the well!
- **Speaker A (Citizen)**: Pass the wet tarps to cover the stable roof!

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Firefighter)**: Evacuate the house! The main beam is cracking!
- **Speaker B (Resident)**: My dog is still inside!

### 14.2 Shipwreck & Crash Survival
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Navigator)**: We hit the reef! The hull is breached!
- **Speaker B (Captain)**: Abandon ship! Launch the lifeboats! Save the navigational logs!
- **Speaker A (Navigator)**: The water is rising in the hold! Hurry!

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Pilot)**: Hold on! The thrusters are failing! We are going down!
- **Speaker B (Copilot)**: Brace for impact! Secure your helmets!

### 14.3 Bandit Raids & Assaults
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Sentry)**: Raiders! They are crossing the north fence! They have torches!
- **Speaker B (Villager)**: Lock the children in the cellar! Get the pitchforks and bows!
- **Speaker A (Sentry)**: They are lighting the barn! Stand and defend!

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Guardian)**: Run to the woods! Do not look back!
- **Speaker B (Child)**: I'm scared, father!

### 14.4 Natural Disasters
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Villager 1)**: The ground is shaking! Get out of the stone buildings!
- **Speaker B (Villager 2)**: The chimney is falling! Stand under the door frame!
- **Speaker A (Villager 1)**: Run to the open field! Keep away from the walls!

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Lookout)**: The volcano is venting ash! The lava is flowing down the east slope!
- **Speaker B (Mayor)**: Sound the evacuation horn. We must leave the valley immediately.

### 14.5 Calling Reinforcements
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Soldier)**: The guard towers are overwhelmed! We cannot hold the wall!
- **Speaker B (Lieutenant)**: Ride to the garrison! Tell the General we need two cohorts immediately!
- **Speaker A (Soldier)**: I am going now! Keep the gate closed!

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Scout)**: Signal with the beacon fires! Light the third pile!
- **Speaker B (Sentry)**: The wood is wet, but it is smoking now.

### 14.6 Search & Rescue
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Rescuer 1)**: Hello! Is anyone trapped under these stones? Can you hear me?
- **Speaker B (Victim)**: Help! Down here! My leg is pinned by a wooden beam.
- **Speaker A (Rescuer 1)**: We hear you! Stay still. We are clearing the rubble now.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Searcher)**: Find the tracks. The child wandered into the forest before the storm.
- **Speaker B (Hunter)**: Here... a broken twig and a small footprint.

### 14.7 Evacuation Coordination
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Officer)**: Everyone, gather at the town square! Pack only what you can carry. Leave the carts behind.
- **Speaker B (Citizen)**: Where are they taking us, officer?
- **Speaker A (Officer)**: To the mountain fortress. The pass is still open. Move quickly!

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Mother)**: Keep the children together. Hold hands!
- **Speaker B (Son)**: I have my sister's hand, mother.

### 14.8 Emergency Medical Triage
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Medic)**: This soldier has arterial bleeding. Apply the tourniquet now!
- **Speaker B (Assistant)**: The tourniquet is secure. What about the miner with the head wound?
- **Speaker A (Medic)**: Bandage it and keep him conscious. Do not let him sleep.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Responder)**: Is there a heartbeat?
- **Speaker B (Medic)**: Yes, but his breathing is shallow. Elevate his legs.

### 14.9 Escape Routes & Avoiding Capture
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Fugitive 1)**: The guards are searching the main street. They have lanterns.
- **Speaker B (Fugitive 2)**: Down here, through the cellar grating. It leads to the sewers.
- **Speaker A (Fugitive 1)**: The grating is rusted. Help me pull it loose.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Companion)**: Quiet! I hear boots on the cobblestones.
- **Speaker B (Traveler)**: Duck behind this wall. Hold your breath.

### 14.10 Signaling for Help
#### Conversation 1
- **Speaker A (Sailor)**: Send up the flare! The rescue ship is passing our island.
- **Speaker B (Mate)**: The signal fire is burning green. They see us! They are turning!
- **Speaker A (Sailor)**: Wave the white banner so they know our position.

#### Conversation 2
- **Speaker A (Climber)**: Blow the whistle three times. The sound will carry through the canyon.
- **Speaker B (Partner)**: I am blowing... hope they hear.
