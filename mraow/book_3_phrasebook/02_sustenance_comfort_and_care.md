# Phrasebook 2: Sustenance, Comfort, Dining & Healing Care

**Practical Conversations around Meals, Rest, and Recovery in Dual Dialects**

---

## Dialogue 1: Sharing a Feast

**Context**: Companion A invites Companion B to share fresh fish/prey and spring water.

### 1. Host
- **Mraow-Trill**: *Trrrt! Prīna nō, tū nā-trr? Prēl lēp-trr?*
- **Mraow-Ruh**: *Tùruht! Purīna nō, tū nā-tur? Purēl lēp-tur?*
- **IPA**: `/trːt purina noː tuː naːtrː prel leːptrː/`
- **Gloss**: `welcome! feast.BEN TOP, 2SG eat-HORT? spring_water lap-HORT?`
- **Translation**: *"Welcome! Would you like to share this fresh meal with me? Shall we drink fresh spring water?"*

### 2. Guest
- **Mraow-Trill**: *Prrr! Ī prīna nā-prr; prēl lēp-prr.*
- **Mraow-Ruh**: *Puruh! Ī purīna nā-pur; purēl lēp-pur.*
- **Gloss**: `content! 1SG feast eat-BEN; spring_water lap-BEN`
- **Translation**: *"Yes, gladly! I will happily enjoy this feast and drink the sweet water."*

## Dialogue 2: Tending to an Injured or Fatigued Companion

### 1. Healer / Caregiver
- **Mraow-Trill**: *Mrrr... Tū shē-mrr. Prōm nō, skē nī fō-hss.*
- **Mraow-Ruh**: *Muruh... Tū shē-mur. Purōm nō, skē nī fō-hss.*
- **Gloss**: `affection... 2SG rest-BEN. den.BEN TOP, pain NEG approach-PROH`
- **Translation**: *"Hush now... Rest gently. In this safe den, pain cannot reach you."*

### 2. Recovering Companion
- **Mraow-Trill**: *H’m... Ī shē-pō. Prrr.*
- **Mraow-Ruh**: *H’m... Ī shē-pō. Puruh.*
- **Gloss**: `silent_trust... 1SG sleep-PFV. contentment`
- **Translation**: *"Thank you in silence... I am drifting to peaceful sleep. Purr."*
