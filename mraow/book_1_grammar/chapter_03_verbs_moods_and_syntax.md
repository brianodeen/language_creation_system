# Chapter 3: Verbs, Aspect Markers, Verbal Moods, and Syntax

Mraow uses an efficient, highly expressive syntactic architecture combining **Topic-Comment structure** with **Aspect Clitics** and **Valence-Driven Mood Suffixes**.

---

## 1. Sentence Architecture: Topic-Comment & S-V-O

A standard sentence in Mraow opens with the **Topic** (marked by particle `nō` or initial position), establishing the focus of discourse, followed by the **Subject**, **Verb Head**, and **Object**:

$$\textbf{[Topic]} \quad (\text{nō}) \quad + \quad \textbf{[Subject]} \quad + \quad \textbf{[Verb Stem - Aspect - Mood]} \quad + \quad \textbf{[Object]}$$

### Examples:
- **Trill**: *Prīna nō, ī nā-prr.*
- **Ruh**: *Purīna nō, ī nā-pur.*
- **Gloss**: `feast TOP, 1SG eat-BEN`
- **Meaning**: *"As for the feast, I eat it with great pleasure."*

---

## 2. Aspect Clitics (Sensory Metaphors)

Verbs in Mraow express aspect through felid predatory and resting gestures:

1. **Prowling Aspect (`-chā` / Imperfective)**: The action is currently in progress, actively stalked.
   - *Ī chā-kkk* = *"I am calculating / currently stalking."*
2. **Pouncing Aspect (`-pō` / Perfective)**: The action landed decisively; completed.
   - *Klān fō-pō* = *"The clan has arrived."*
3. **Basking Aspect (`-lā` / Habitual)**: The action occurs regularly like basking in a daily beam of sun.
   - *Ī shē-lā* = *"I regularly rest / sleep here."*

---

## 3. Verbal Mood Clitics (Emotional Stance)

The speaker's emotional commitment and social intent are encoded at the end of the verb phrase:

| Suffix (Trill / Ruh) | Feline Origin | Grammatical Mood | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`-prr`** / **`-pur`** | Purr | **Benedictive** | *"Happily do / love doing X"* |
| **`-trr`** / **`-tur`** | Chirp | **Hortative** | *"Let us do X together / please do X"* |
| **`-mrr`** / **`-mur`** | Churr | **Affectionate** | *"Gently care for / soothe X"* |
| **`-hss`** / **`-hss`** | Hiss | **Prohibitive** | *"Do NOT do X / I reject X"* |
| **`-grr`** / **`-gur`** | Growl | **Imperative** | *"Must do X by territorial decree"* |
| **`-kkk`** / **`-klik`** | Chatter | **Intentive** | *"Aiming / calculating to achieve X"* |
