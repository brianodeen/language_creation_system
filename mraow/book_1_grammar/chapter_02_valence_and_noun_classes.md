# Chapter 2: The Valence System & Noun Classes

In Mraow, reality is perceived through the acoustic and survival lens of **Axiological Valence**: how entities and phenomena relate to comfort, safety, nourishment, and survival versus threat, hostility, and poison.

---

## 1. The Four Valence Noun Classes

Every noun in Mraow belongs to one of four classes, marked either by intrinsic root semantics or derivational prefixes and infixes:

### Class I: Purr-Beneficial (`pr-` / `pur-`)
Entities that bring warmth, safety, healing, and life:
- `praow` / `purāow` — Sunbeam, pleasant basking weather
- `prīna` / `purīna` — Wholesome food, nourishing feast
- `prōm` / `purōm` — Safe den, secure hearth
- `prēl` / `purēl` — Fresh spring water

### Class II: Hiss-Hazardous (`hs-` / `sf-`)
Entities that bring poison, danger, hostility, and misery:
- `hsaow` — Violent storm, freezing hail, blizzard
- `hsīna` — Poison, spoiled food, toxic bait
- `hsōm` — Cage, prison, hostile trap
- `hsēl` — Stagnant foul water, acid

### Class III: Growl-Territorial (`gr-` / `gur-`)
Entities representing heavy mass, physical barriers, bedrock, and laws:
- `graow` / `gùraow` — Mountain wind, high ridge gale
- `grōm` / `gùrōm` — Stone citadel, fortress, clan hall
- `grāow` / `gùraow` — Territorial border, bedrock jurisdiction

### Class IV: Meow-Neutral (`mr-` / `m-`)
Common tools, celestial bodies, abstract units of time, and baseline objects:
- `mrāow` / `məráow` — Baseline object, standard entity
- `kōr` — Star, celestial beacon
- `nyā-kē` — Claws, sharp instruments

---

## 2. Derivational Valence Shifting

Speakers can take an abstract root and systematically shift its valence to generate precise vocabulary:

$$\text{Root: [aow (atmosphere)]} \implies \begin{cases} \text{Purr-Beneficial:} & \textbf{pr-aow} \quad (\text{warm sunbeam, fine day}) \\ \text{Hiss-Hazardous:} & \textbf{hs-aow} \quad (\text{blizzard, violent hail}) \\ \text{Growl-Territorial:} & \textbf{gr-aow} \quad (\text{mountain gale, ridge wind}) \\ \text{Meow-Neutral:} & \textbf{mr-aow} \quad (\text{sky, ambient weather}) \end{cases}$$
