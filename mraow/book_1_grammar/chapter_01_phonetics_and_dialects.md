# Chapter 1: The Sounds of Mraow, Tone Contours, and Dual Dialects

Welcome to **Mraow**, the human-spoken language derived from feline vocalizations, prosodic pitch modulations, and natural acoustic valences.

---

## 1. The Feline Sound Palette

Mraow organizes natural cat sounds into 7 distinct acoustic families. For human speakers, these are articulated through standard vocal gestures:

1. **Meow/Mraow (`mrāow`)**: Open, gliding vocalic call. Used for standard greetings, naming objects, asking questions, and stating baseline reality.
2. **Purr (`prrr` / `puruh`)**: Continuous laryngeal vibration (or alveolar trill / accessible vocalic rumble). Signifies deep safety, contentment, restorative healing, and enthusiastic affirmation (*"yes"*).
3. **Chirp/Trill (`trrrt` / `tùruht`)**: High ascending rolling sound. Used for friendly approaches, invitations, and delighted discoveries.
4. **Churr/Murmur (`mrrr` / `muruh`)**: Gentle low-frequency vibration. Signifies maternal affection and soothing touch.
5. **Hiss (`hss`)**: Voiceless alveolar fricative. Signifies defensive rejection, boundary protection, toxicity, and categorical refusal (*"no"*).
6. **Spit (`pft` / `k’a`)**: Explosive burst. Used as an immediate reflex command to abort hazardous actions.
7. **Growl (`grrr` / `gùruh`)**: Deep guttural rumble. Signifies territorial law, physical weight, and commanding dominance.

## 2. The Dual-Dialect System: Trill vs. Ruh

To ensure Mraow can be spoken comfortably by everyone—including those who cannot produce an alveolar rolled *R*—the language exists in two fully recognized, mutually intelligible dialects:

| Concept | Mraow-Trill (Classical Felid) | Mraow-Ruh (Human-Accessible) | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Greeting** | `Mrāow` `/mraʊ⁵⁵/` | `Məráow` `/məˈraʊ⁵⁵/` | Hello / baseline existence |
| **Affirmation ("Yes")** | `Prrr` `/prː/` | `Puruh` `/ˈpʊ.rʌ/` | Yes / deep contentment |
| **Welcome** | `Trrrt!` `/trːt/` | `Tùruht!` `/ˈtʊ.rʌt/` | Welcome! Come join us! |
| **Affection** | `Mrrr` `/mrː/` | `Muruh` `/ˈmʊ.rʌ/` | Soothing affection |
| **Law / Border** | `Grrr` `/grː/` | `Gùruh` `/ˈgʊ.rʌ/` | Territorial law / authority |

## 3. The 5 Pitch Tones

Every vowel in Mraow carries one of 5 pitch contours that modulate the pragmatic and grammatical stance:

1. **Level Tone 1 (`¯` / Macron)**: Factual statement, static existence (`Mrāow`).
2. **Rising Tone 2 (`´` / Acute)**: Question, polite inquiry, seeking entry (`Mráow?`).
3. **Falling Tone 3 (``` ` ``` / Grave)**: Command, finality, decisive halt (`Mràow!`).
4. **Dipping Tone 4 (`ˇ` / Caron)**: Playfulness, hunting stalking, conditional possibility (`Prřt`).
5. **Peaking Tone 5 (`ˆ` / Circumflex)**: Surprise, sudden alarm, revelation (`Yâowl`).
